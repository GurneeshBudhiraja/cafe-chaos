CAFÉ CHAOS

Sit at a table. Put down your controllers.

How to play

Sit at a table.
The café stands on the table.
Put down your controllers.
Use only your hands.

1Pinch a guest

Put your fingers above a guest at the gate. When the guest glows, pinch.

2Seat the guest

Move the guest above the café. Open your fingers. The guest goes to the seat that glows.

3Keep guests happy

Seat guests quickly. The bar above the café shows how happy the guests are.
