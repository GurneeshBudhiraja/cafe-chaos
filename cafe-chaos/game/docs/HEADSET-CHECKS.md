# Checks only the headset can make

These cannot be confirmed in the emulator. After each phase lands, a
few minutes with the headset on answers them. The most useful things to
report are in bold.

## Any time
- **The frame rate** (the Quest's performance overlay, or how smooth the
  hands look against the café): steady, or does it stutter when the café
  is full?
- Put the café on two or three different tables or desks. Does it land
  on the surface, upright, at a sensible size, every time?
- Take the headset off in the middle of a run and put it back on. Is the
  game paused, and does Resume carry on where it was?

## Hands (phase 3)
- Your real hand in front of the café: is there a rim of the room around
  it, or does the café creep over your fingertips?
- **Picking up. When it fails, which is it:** nothing lights up; the
  wrong guest lights up; it lights up but the pinch does not take it; it
  takes it and then drops it?
- Carrying: does the guest shake in a still hand, or trail behind a
  moving one?
- Letting go: does the guest go where you meant?

## The side board (phase 2)
- Can you read the clock and the coins without leaning?
- Can you press Hire and Pause with a fingertip without reaching past
  the café? Do you ever press them by accident?

## The start (phase 4)
- From putting the headset on to seating the first guest: how long, and
  did you have to read anything twice?

## The room (phase 8)
- Do guests walking across your table look like they are on it (not
  floating above, not sunk into it)?
